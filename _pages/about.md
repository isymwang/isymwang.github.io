<p><span data-lang="en">My research focuses on neural video coding (NVC) and feature coding for machines (FCM). I have published 20+ papers.</span><span data-lang="zh">我的研究方向为神经视频编码（NVC）和面向机器的特征编码（FCM），已发表学术论文 20 余篇。</span></p>

<p><span data-lang="en">I received my Ph.D. in <strong>Computer Science and Technology</strong> from the <strong>School of Computer Science and Software Engineering, Hohai University</strong> in 2025, supervised by <a href="https://cies.hhu.edu.cn/2024/0520/c21523a280042/page.htm">Prof. Bin Tang</a> and <a href="https://cies.hhu.edu.cn/2024/0521/c21522a280102/page.htm">Prof. Qian Huang</a>.</span><span data-lang="zh">我于 2025 年在<strong>河海大学计算机与软件学院</strong>获得<strong>计算机科学与技术博士学位</strong>，导师为 <a href="https://cies.hhu.edu.cn/2024/0520/c21523a280042/page.htm">唐斌教授</a>和 <a href="https://cies.hhu.edu.cn/2024/0521/c21522a280102/page.htm">黄倩老师</a>。</span></p>

<p><span data-lang="en">In 2024, I was a research intern at the Multimedia Lab, ByteDance Inc., collaborating with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ffdIhlEAAAAJ&hl=en">Yaojun Wu</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=p_GmN9YAAAAJ&hl=en">Zhaobin Zhang</a> under the guidance of <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5rhIVxQAAAAJ&hl=en">Kai Zhang</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8G5-2OMAAAAJ&hl=en">Li Zhang</a>.</span><span data-lang="zh">2024 年，我在字节跳动多媒体实验室开展科研实习，在 <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5rhIVxQAAAAJ&hl=en">张凯</a> 和 <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8G5-2OMAAAAJ&hl=en">张莉</a> 的指导下，与 <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ffdIhlEAAAAJ&hl=en">吴耀军</a>、<a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=p_GmN9YAAAAJ&hl=en">张召宾</a> 合作开展研究。</span></p>
